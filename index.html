2D vs 3D Cadastral Paradigm

Traditional 2D Failure: Flattened polygon records cannot assign distinct legal ownership to multiple apartment owners stacked on the same ground footprint.
3D Volumetric ULPIN: Adds standardized Z-elevation bounds, floor index, and unit delimiters to the 14-digit Bhu-Aadhaar base standard.
Subsurface Integration: Prevents uncoordinated excavation and property disputes by mapping deep utilities, metro tunnels, and basement easements.
